## Full-length clips

The full clips are not stored in the repository. They are portrait videos,
720x1280, without an audio track.

| Clip | Length | Playback speed | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| `supervisor_cube.mp4` | 11.3 s | real time | cube, with the supervisor |

| `cup_compliant_speed_ramped.mp4` | 12.3 s | speed-ramped | thin-walled cup, compliant control, intact |

"Real time" means the clip is the camera recording at 30 frames per second
with no sign of retiming. The two retimed clips were re-encoded for a
presentation.

A video referenced by repository path does not play inline on GitHub. To get
an inline player, a clip has to be uploaded through the GitHub web editor,
which returns an attachment URL that is then pasted into the README.
